# Returns and Refunds Policy — fictional demo store

These policies are fictional coursework examples, not a real retailer's promises.

## Return eligibility
Unused items, including shoes, in their original packaging can be requested for return within 30 days of delivery. Keep your proof of purchase.

## How to request a return
Contact support with your order number and the item you want to return. A representative will review eligibility and provide return instructions. Do not send items back until the return is approved.

## Refund timing
After an approved returned item is received and inspected, an eligible refund is sent to the original payment method within 5-10 business days. This assistant cannot approve returns or issue refunds.
