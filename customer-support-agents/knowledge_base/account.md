# Account Access Policy — fictional demo store

These policies are fictional coursework examples, not a real retailer's promises.

## Forgotten password
On the sign-in page, select "Forgot password", enter the email address linked to your account, and follow the reset link sent by email. Check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

## Continued sign-in problems
If the reset email does not arrive or the reset link has expired, request a new link. If you still cannot sign in, contact support for identity verification. Never share your password or one-time verification codes in chat.

This assistant cannot access accounts, reset passwords itself, or bypass identity verification.
