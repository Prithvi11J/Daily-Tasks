# Shipping Policy — fictional demo store

These policies are fictional coursework examples, not a real retailer's promises.

## Standard delivery
Standard orders normally arrive within 3-5 business days after dispatch. Delivery estimates are not guarantees.

## Delayed orders
For a delayed order, open the tracking link in your dispatch email and check the latest carrier update. If the expected delivery date has passed, contact support with your order number so a representative can investigate with the carrier.

This assistant cannot look up live tracking, confirm an order's location, contact a carrier, or guarantee an arrival date.
