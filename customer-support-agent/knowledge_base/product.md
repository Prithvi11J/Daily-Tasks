# Damaged and Defective Products Policy — fictional demo store

These policies are fictional coursework examples, not a real retailer's promises.

## Broken product on arrival
Stop using a damaged or defective product. Contact support within 7 days of delivery with your order number, a description of the problem, and photos of the item and packaging.

## Replacement review
A representative will review the evidence and, if the claim is approved, arrange a replacement subject to stock availability or discuss an eligible refund. Keep the damaged item and packaging until you receive instructions.

This assistant cannot approve a replacement, determine stock, or authorize a refund.
